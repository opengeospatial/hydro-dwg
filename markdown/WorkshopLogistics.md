## [Things to do](http://www.koblenz-tourism.com/)

Koblenz offers [numerous attractions](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g187391-Activities-Koblenz_Rhineland_Palatinate.html). It is a favourite tourist destination located at the confluence of the Rhine and Mosel rivers within the UNESCO World Heritage Site ["Upper Middle Rhine Valley"](http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1066). Apart from visiting some interesting spots in town, take a trip with the [cable car](http://www.seilbahn-koblenz.de/home-page.html) across the Rhine to the Fortress Ehrenbreitstein for a stunning view of the [confluence of the Rhine and Moselle rivers](http://www.bafg.de/EN/06_Info_Service/03_Webcam_en/webcam_en_node.html) called "Deutsches Eck". Enjoy a boat trip and/or the world famous wines from [Rhine](http://www.germany.travel/en/towns-cities-culture/gemuetlichkeit/wine-country/winegrowing-regions/middle-rhine.html) and [Moselle](http://www.germany.travel/en/towns-cities-culture/gemuetlichkeit/wine-country/winegrowing-regions/moselle.html) vineyards.
