## **ANNOUNCEMENT**

- **Dates**: 25 - 28 June, 2012
- **Venue:** University of Reading, Reading, UK
- **Purpose:** This meeting will be the third workshop of the OGC\WMO Hydrology Domain Working Group. After very succesful meetings in [Ispra (2010)](http://external.opengis.org/twiki_public/HydrologyDWG/JRCWkShop2010) and [Delft (2011)](http://external.opengis.org/twiki_public/HydrologyDWG/IHEWkShop2011), there are high hopes of great things from this meeting.

## **Related Activities**

### Ratings Workshop, June 20-22

- The HDWG workshop will be preceded by a \<a href="<http://external.opengis.org/twiki_public/HydrologyDWG/RatingsAndGaugingsWorkshopReading>" title="Ratings and Gaugings Intensive"\>Ratings and Gaugings Intensive workshop, June 20-22. ### Groundwater IE2 Breakout Session, June 28

### Site Visit: Center for Ecology and Hydrology, Wallingford, June 27 PM

- An afternoon tour of the facilities with talks.
