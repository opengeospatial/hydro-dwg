# Hydro DWG Workshop Koblenz 2016

- **When:** 13 - 17 June 2016
- **Where:** Federal Institute of Hydrology ([BfG](BfG)), [Am Mainzer Tor 1, Koblenz, Germany](https://www.google.de/maps/place/Bundesanstalt+f%C3%BCr+Gew%C3%A4sserkunde/@50.3535415,7.5980198,18z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0000000000000000:0x3e4b7cf1d45cbb5c?hl=en), Conference Rooms on Second Floor

- **Contact:** Uli Looser at <Looser@bafg.de> or Irina Dornblut at <Dornblut@bafg.de>
